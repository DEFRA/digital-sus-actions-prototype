Digital Sustainability Actions (DSAs)

The Digital Sustainability Actions are a set of guidelines for the design, development and operation of more environmentally sustainable services and other digital projects. That means resilient, circular, efficient services.

For projects in Defra, these actions provide guidance on how teams can deliver on the objectives of the Defra Digital Sustainability Strategy 2025 to 2030. For other departments and public sector bodies they represent advice on sustainability best practice.

The actions have been grouped under 9 themes:

  • Research, planning and tracking progress

    How sustainability is considered in research and planning and impact measured over time

  • Journey & Process Design

    How user journeys and supporting processes are designed

  • Software

    Code and applications that provide digital functionality

  • Data

    How information is collected, processed, stored, transferred and managed

  • Architecture

    How the technology and components of a digital service fit together

  • Artificial Intelligence

    How AI is selected, developed and used within a digital service

  • Hosting

    Where and how a digital service and its data are hosted

  • Hardware

    The physical equipment used to provide, manage and access a digital service

  • Procurement

    How technology and digital services are bought and suppliers managed

The Digital Sustainability Actions have been developed through an open and collaborative process involving colleagues from a wide range of disciplines across UK government and the Government Digital Sustainability Alliance.
