Digital Sustainability Actions (DSAs)

The Digital Sustainability Actions are a set of guidelines for the design, development and operation of more environmentally sustainable services and other digital projects. That means resilient, circular, efficient services.

The actions have been grouped under 9 themes:

  • Research, planing and measurement

    Design services and the systems they depend on.

  • Journey & Process Design

    Analyse business problems and recommend solutions.

  • Software

    Apply circular principles

  • Data

    Guidance for Service and Interaction Designers at Defra.

  • Architecture

    Measure how services perform and turn data into insight.

  • Arttificial Intelligence

    Lead the vision, roadmap and day-to-day delivery of services.

  • Hosting

    Check services work, are accessible and meet standards.

  • Hardware

    Build, test and deploy services at Defra.

  • Procurement

    Find out what users need and how services perform.

More information on the background and shape of these actions can be found on the about page.

Divided into 9 themes, these actions have been developed through an open and collaborative process involving colleagues from a wide range of disciplines across UK government and beyond.
