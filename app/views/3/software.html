3. Software

The way that software is designed, developed, tested and operated determines how much computing, data storage, network capacity and hardware are needed to deliver a digital service. Software code may be intangible but when it runs it's use translates into physical impacts across energy use, water, and a range of embodied impacts coming from the whole lifecycle of hardware.

Applying the Digital Sustainability Actions for software

The actions in this software section of the Digital Sustainability actions are primary aimed at the development, testing and operation of bespoke software that is developed in or for government.

Where software is procured ‘as a service’, suppliers can be asked for evidence for how far their applications adhere to the actions.

Software development standards in Defra

All developers working on behalf of Defra need to follow the Defra Software Development Standards.

More guidance can be found in the Software Development section of the Defra Service Manual.
