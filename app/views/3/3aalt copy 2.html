Sustainability Action

3a. Take a lean approach to data collection

Ensuring that only data that has value for user needs or business requirements is captured, has not been captured elsewhere and opportunities for sharing are exploited reduces data stored and assoicated energy use.

Lead role families: Data Product
Likely co-benefits Cost: Lower costs coming the storage of data

How to deliver on this action

The following points enable delivery of this action:

(i) Re-use existing data where possible, and only collect new data where necessary

Establish user or organisational needs for data collection, identify if other services or projects, in your department or perhaps adjacent ones, have already collected the data that your service needs.

Co-benefits and trade-offs

Co-benefits

Alongside sustainability benefits, setting a page weight budget brings co-benefits in terms of:

  • Inclusivity: Budgets encourage close consideration of what is essential for users with small screen sizes or low bandwidth

(ii) Implement an appropriate data retention policy

A data retention policy defines how long data must be kept for legal or operational purposes, and how it can be deleted.

Ensuring that data is removed when no longer needed prevents accumulation of redundant or unused data, reducing long term data storage and energy consumption.
