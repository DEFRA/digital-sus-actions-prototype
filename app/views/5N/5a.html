5a. Be clear on the need and readiness for AI use

Ensuring that there is clarity on the value that the use of AI technologies will bring to a project ensures that resources are not wasted, when an alternative approach could produce equal or better outcomes.

(i) Be clear on the use case for AI

Not every problem needs an AI technology as a solution. Consider whether AI is necessary for what you're trying to achieve, what the benefits are that will come from AI use, and if there's a simpler approach that would work just as well.

Questions to consider are:

  • What function will AI perform - will it collect data, automate tasks, generate new content?
  • Is generative AI required, or could non-generative AI such as image recognition or natural language processing be more appropriate?
  • What additional benefit does AI deliver compared to non-AI technologies or processes?
