9a. Procurement and sustainability

For general procurement guidance, follow the GCA https://www.gca.gov.uk/

There are a number of sustainability standards and guidance to be embedded into procurement.

The Supplier shall remain aligned with the latest government guidance and demonstrate proactive compliance as new versions are released.

Mandatory sustainability standards and practice for procurement

There are a number of UK government standards that are required to guide the sustainable procurement of technology and other service aspects.

Governmental strategies and commitments on sustainability

The Supplier must ensure that the provision of services supports the Buyers efforts to achieve and comply with:

Define each of the below?

  • Greening government: ICT and digital services strategy 2020 to 2025, and any future updates or replacement strategies, including the anticipated Government Digital Sustainability Strategy
  • Greening government commitments 2021 to 2025, and any subsequent commitments or frameworks that replace or extend them

The Government Buying Standard

The UK's Government Buying Standards (GBS) set mandatory minimum sustainability criteria for central government procurement of goods and services.

Where Government Buying Standards do not exist for a particular technology category, the Buyer encourages the Supplier to have regard to recognised sustainability standards and frameworks, such as Green Public Procurement criteria, Energy Star, TCO Certified, EPEAT or equivalent schemes.

Updated Government Buying Standards focus on 4 categories of digital

When designing, implementing and delivering services, suppliers must adopt the applicable elements of the following guidance and standards:

  • The Technology Code of Practice is a set of criteria to help government design, build and buy technology. Point 12 of the Tech Code of Practice is focused on sustainable technology.
  • The GOV.UK Service Standard is a set of 14 principles for design, develop and operate high-quality digital and end-to-end services

Enbedding the Digital Sustainability Actions when designing, implementing and delivering services

The Supplier should (when designing, implementing and delivering the Services) adopt the applicable elements of the Digital Sustainability Actions and demonstrate which actions have been met and how they have been delivered

As best practice, suppliers are encourage to adopt the Digital Sustainability Actions when designing, implementing and delivery government services. To aid the implementation of the DSAs you should ask potential bidders about their intention, ability and action to deliver the DSAs through market engagement, specifications, tender questions and evaluation. You can further embed the DSAs by calling out specific actions and integrating them into non-functional requirements, KPIs and contract clauses
