9. Procurement

Procurement decisions can significantly influence sustainability by determining the environmental and social impacts of the products, services and supply chains government supports, including their carbon emissions, resource use, waste and wider lifecycle impacts.

Procurement may often occur at the departmental level, and so be out of the control of an individual service or project delivery team. However, in some circumstances procurement may be influenced by projects teams or occur once project delivery has begun.

Procurement frameworks and guidance in UK Government

There are a number of frameworks and sources of guidance for conducting procurement in UK Government. These include:

  • The Government Commercial Agency (GCA) website provides guidance, commercial agreements, supplier information and procurement tools to help UK public sector organisations buy goods and services effectively and achieve value for the nation
  • The Procurement Act 2023 guidance documents provide technical guidance for public procurement practitioners on interpreting and applying the Procurement Act 2023 and associated regulations across the planning, defining, procuring and contract management stages of the commercial lifecycle
