7c. Choose providers that can deliver on sustainability at the service level

Choosing providers that are managing sustainability.

(i) Choose providers offering service-level sustainability reporting

Hosting providers can report on energy use, carbon, water and heat re-use at a number of levels. These can range from the facility as a whole down to individual servers and software services, allowing operators to track efficiency and allocate costs.

Choose providers who are able to report on metrics at the level of an individual service or project. This can enable insight into where efficiencies can be found and optimisation made.

(ii) Choose providers that offer the ability to maximise server utilisation and compute efficiency

There are a range of measures, such as right-sizing and autoscaling that can be taken to maximise the efficiency of computing activities in data centres.

Choose suppliers that offer service level optimisation in order to achieve high server utilisation & compute efficiency.

See also: 5b. Ensure cloud services are efficiently provisioned and 5d. Maximise efficiency in on-premises data centres

(iii) Choose providers with a plan to manage hardware sustainably at the end of its usable life

Disposing of hardware sustainably means choosing options that are at the 'top' of the waste hierarchy (such as re-use) over those further down (such as recycling).

Select providers that have a plan for the sustainble management of any hardware that needs to be retired.
