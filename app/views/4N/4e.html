4e. Maximise efficiency in on-premises data centres

Take action to ensure that on-premises servers are running as efficiently as possible, reducing associated energy consumption.

An on-premises data centre is a physical computing facility owned, built, and managed directly by an organization within its own building or property

Lead role families: Architecture
Likely co-benefits Cost: Lower costs coming the storage of data

(i) Consolidate workloads

Consolidating workloads means combining multiple applications, databases, or virtual machines that previously ran on separate physical servers onto a smaller number of higher capacity servers.

This approach can increase hardware utilisation rates and reduce overall energy consumption.

(ii) Implement dynamic balancing

Dynamic balancing involves distributing network traffic in real-time across local servers based on current demand.

This approach can optimise power distribution and reduce overall energy consumption.
