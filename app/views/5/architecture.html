Cloud-first policy in UK government

Many digital services have shifted to being hosted in the public cloud.

Public cloud is a model where a third-party provider delivers computing resources over the public internet to multiple customers who share the same underlying hardware infrastructure

https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cloud-guide-for-the-public-sector/cloud-guide-for-the-public-sector

'Cloud first' policy from 2013, https://www.gov.uk/guidance/government-cloud-first-policy/ 'Organisations who do not deploy in Public Cloud should ensure they can evidence the decision, business case and value for money behind their choice.'

https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/cloud-technology-and-the-public-sector
