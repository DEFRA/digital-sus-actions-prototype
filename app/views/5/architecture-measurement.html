Measurement for Architecture

Measuring the environmental impacts of service architecture can:

  • Provide practioners with insights into environmental impact hotspots , and thus how to reduce over all impacts
  • Be integrated into broader whole-service measurement, alongside other aspects of digital services or projects

The primary metric to measure is energy use, which can then be used to estimate operational carbon emissions. Water consumption can also be estimated using additional data-centre water efficiency metrics Other impacts, such as the embodied carbon of infrastructure, should be considered where possible.

For architecture, I think useful to say that:
  • Architects may find it useful to look at specific components of a service, rather than the total figures
  • Usage from cloud providers dashboards, usage reports and monitoring tools can be used as a proxy to guide architectural decisions
  • Otherwise, direct reader to the main measurement page for estimating energy in cloud or on- and off-premises data centres
