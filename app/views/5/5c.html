5c. Shift cloud demand to match availability of low carbon electricity

Being 'carbon aware' means scheduling and running cloud workloads at times and in places when more renewable energy is available on the electricity grid.

The UK electricity grid’s carbon intensity varies with the time of day and weather conditions. There are also regional differences with the ‘mix’ of energy sources available varying significantly between parts of the country. ‘Carbon aware’ digital services are able to respond to shifts in the carbon intensity of the electricity grid.

NB, shifting workload locations may not be possible in government due to contractual and/or data sovereignty reasons.

Lead role families: Architecture
Likely co-benefits Cost: Lower costs coming the storage of data

(i) Shift workloads in time

The UK electricity grid tends to have its highest mix of renewable electricity late at night and early in the morning, as well as on bright, windy, days.

Where possible, shift non-critical workloads (like AI training, batch processing, or data backups) to times when there is more renewable electricity available.

This is often called ‘temporal demand shifting.’

See also 4a. Design for sustainable architectural patterns

(ii) Shift workload location

The proportion of renewable energy available on the electricty grid varies significantly between UK regions, depending on methods of local electricity generation and distribution between regions.

Where possible, shift workloads from regions with a lower proportion of renewables available to one where they are higher.

This is often called ‘spatial demand shifting.’
