(i) Ai model pruning

General-purpose AI models (such as Large Language Models) are trained on broad datasets can perform a variety of tasks, such as coding, generating text or images.

In contrast, task-specific models are those built and fine-tuned for a single, well-defined function and trained on specific datasets.

Co-benefits and trade-offs

Co-benefits

Alongside sustainability impact, choosing task-specific models brings co-benefits in terms of:

  • Accuracy: Accuracy and Precision: Task-specific models often deliver higher domain accuracy and lower hallucination rates
  • Governance: Task-specific models make it straightforward to apply strict compliance, safety guardrails, and drift detection.

Trade-offs

Potential trade-offs may include:

  • Operational process: Introduction of operational overhead for monitoring, alerting and tuning cloud usage
